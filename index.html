Development Pipeline

From printed page to production order.

  1. Read

    Readers search for visible wants, clean conflicts, useful settings, and a protagonist worth filming.

  2. Mine

    Promising material is broken into hooks, perils, reversals, attractions, and screenable business.

  3. Conference

    Story editors argue sympathy, morality, comedy, romance, spectacle, and commercial force.

  4. Treat

    The winning premise becomes a treatment with acts, set pieces, emotional stakes, and a verdict.

Carstairs' Office

The final question is never whether it is literary. It is whether it moves.

A story may be elegant, clever, fashionable, or shocking. But the studio only buys what can become action under pressure, emotion with consequence, and a final image the public will carry home.
