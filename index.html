Production Pipeline

From printed page to production order.

  1. Intake

    Log title, source, premise, genre, promise, danger, and the one thing the department must not miss.

  2. Report

    Readers search for visible wants, clean conflicts, useful settings, and a protagonist worth filming.

  3. Conference

    Story editors argue sympathy, morality, comedy, romance, spectacle, and commercial force.

  4. Treat

    The winning premise becomes a treatment with acts, set pieces, emotional stakes, and a verdict.

  5. Verdict

    Carstairs weighs money, stars, sets, costumes, and the final image the audience will carry home.
