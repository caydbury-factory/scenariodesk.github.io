Synopsis - As Prepared by the Scenario Reader

Beatrice "Bee" Vance - High-society romances and modern audience appeal

Barbara Ferrers, a spirited lady's maid, dreams of a dazzling adventure aboard a luxury Mediterranean cruise, where sunlight decks shimmer and laughter dances on the breeze. Her life takes an unexpected turn when a mysterious stranger saves her from a perilous fall, sparking a forbidden romance that defies their class boundaries. Torn between loyalty to her station and her desire for freedom, Barbara must decide whether love is worth the scandal that follows.
